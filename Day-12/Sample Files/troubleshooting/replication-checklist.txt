## S3 Replication Troubleshooting Checklist

1. Confirm **S3 Versioning** is enabled on both the source and destination buckets.
2. Confirm the **replication rule is enabled** and correctly configured.
3. Verify that the object key matches the configured **prefix or tag-based filter**.
4. Confirm the object was uploaded **after the replication rule became active**.
5. Check the source object's **replication status** for the current version.
6. Confirm the replication **IAM role** has permission to read from the source bucket and write to the destination bucket.
7. For **SSE-KMS encrypted objects**, verify that the required source and destination **KMS key permissions** are correctly configured.
8. Confirm the **destination bucket and AWS Region** are correct.
9. Remember that S3 replication is **asynchronous**, so replication may remain **Pending** temporarily.
10. Use **S3 Batch Replication** when eligible pre-existing objects need to be replicated.
